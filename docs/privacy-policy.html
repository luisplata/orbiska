Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Orbiska ("the game", "we", "our") collects and how it is handled.

The game is published by Bellseboss Studio and is available as:

  • A free WebGL game in the browser.
  • A free Android build (APK) offered on itch.io.
  • A free Android release on Google Play.

1. Information We Collect

We Collect No Personal Data

As of the effective date above, Orbiska does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Specifically:

  • No profiles or sign-in — there is no login, registration, or user profile system.
  • No analytics — we do not use analytics SDKs, trackers, or crash reporting services that collect personal data.
  • No advertising — the game contains no third-party ads.
  • No location data — we do not access or request your device location.
  • No purchase or payment data — the game is free and contains no in-app purchases.
  • No contact or address book data — we do not access your contacts or device files.

The game runs fully offline and does not transmit any data outside your device. Your gameplay session stays entirely on your device, and nothing is kept after you close the game.

2. Children's Privacy

We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children. The game has no sign-in, chat, messaging, or any feature that would require personal data from players of any age. It is designed to be COPPA-friendly: no registration, no data collection, and no targeted advertising.

Because no personal information is collected from any player, there is no children's data for us to delete or disclose.

3. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties. Because no personal data is collected, there is nothing to share.

The only third-party services involved are the platforms that host and offer the game (web hosting, itch.io, and Google Play). These platforms operate under their own privacy policies.

4. Data Security

Since no personal data is collected, there is no user data stored on our servers to protect. If data collection is added in the future, this section will be updated to describe the security measures in place.

5. Contact Us

Email: support@bellseboss.com

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
